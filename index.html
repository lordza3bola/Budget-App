Import bank statement (PDF)

Reads transactions straight out of an HSBC-style PDF statement — charges become transactions, credits/transfers become payments, and the credit limit, statement day and due day are filled in from the statement itself. Already-imported transactions are skipped automatically if you import the same statement twice.

When a bank message arrives, your Shortcut sends it to the Kashu script. A vendor with a rule here is added straight away; anything else (and every IPN transfer) asks you for the category. A new vendor you categorize is saved here with Always ask on, so it keeps asking — switch that off once a vendor is always the same category.

A credit card (a card below that goes to a Mastercard profile) has its own rule list. Its charges are filed by those rules, payments to it are added with no question, and charges in USD are estimated in EGP from the drop in available limit — importing the statement PDF later puts in the bank's exact amounts.
