Credit limit & billing

Import bank statement (PDF)

Reads transactions straight out of an HSBC-style PDF statement — charges become transactions, credits/transfers become payments, and the credit limit, statement day and due day are filled in from the statement itself. Already-imported transactions are skipped automatically if you import the same statement twice.

Excel sync

Import replaces the chosen profile's expenses, budget lines and income with what's in the file (that profile's categories are kept) — export first if you want a backup of anything entered only in the app.

Export to Excel

Leave both dates blank to export everything, or set one or both to export just that range.

Cloud Sync

Demo data

Adds three sample profiles (a regular budget, a credit card, and a savings account) with a few months of realistic-looking data, so you can show the app to someone without using your real numbers.

Data

This erases the expenses, budget lines, income and categories in the current profile only — other profiles are untouched. Export to Excel first if you want to keep a copy.

Delete profile

Removes the whole "current" tab, including its card info. You'll be switched to another profile. This can't be undone.
